Questions people ask before booking

Do you bring cleaning materials and equipment?

Yes. Deep cleans, move-in/out cleans and carpet and sofa cleaning always include equipment and materials. For hourly cleaners you can choose either way. The estimator shows both prices.

Is the estimate the final price?

It's a starting price for a typical home of that size. We confirm the exact price on WhatsApp before booking. Heavy stains, very large rooms or extra requests can change it, and we'll always tell you first.

How long does a deep clean take?

A 1–2 bed apartment usually takes most of a day with a small team. Villas take longer or get a bigger team. We'll give you a time when we confirm.

Do I need to be at home?

No. Many customers leave a key with security or reception. Just let us know access details when you book.

How long do carpets and sofas take to dry?

Usually a few hours, depending on the fabric and how much ventilation and AC the room has.

Do you clean the outside of villas?

Yes. We wash facades, boundary walls and gates, pressure wash driveways and patios, and clean terraces, roofs, pool decks and outdoor furniture. Send a few photos on WhatsApp and we'll quote.

Can you clean our office outside working hours?

Tell us the hours that suit you, whether early morning, evening or weekend, and we'll confirm the schedule with your quote. We do one-off cleans and regular contracts.

Can you clean every week?

Yes. Book a regular hourly cleaner on the same day and time each week. Tell us on WhatsApp and we'll set it up.
